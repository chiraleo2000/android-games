## Data stays on the device

Volume, language, visual settings, the last instrument you opened, and error notes are stored only on the device. The app does not create accounts. It does not show ads. It does not send analytics or recordings off the device. It does not collect personal content in its logs.

The app does not use the microphone. Tones are synthesized on the device.

## Clear data

Settings includes Clear All Data. After you confirm, saved preferences and the last instrument are erased and are not restored.
