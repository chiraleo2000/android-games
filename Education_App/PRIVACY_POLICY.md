# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how [LEGAL_NAME] ("we") handles information in the LogicLabs app (package `com.logiclabs.codeescape`).

## On-device progress only

LogicLabs stores game progress on your device. That includes the highest level you have reached, stars, achievements, settings such as the sound toggle, and whether you finished the tutorial. Code programs you build stay in the app while you play. They are not logged or transmitted off the device.

## No account, no ads, no off-device transmission

- We do not ask you to create an account.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- We do not sell or share personal information.
- The app does not transmit programs, scores, progress, settings, or any other player information off the device.

## Android INTERNET permission

The Android package includes the `INTERNET` permission because the Capacitor Android WebView loads the bundled game from a local `https://localhost` address inside the app. Android requires that permission for the WebView's local loader. The permission is not used to contact our servers or any third party, and the game is playable without a network connection.

## Children

LogicLabs is a general-audience educational game. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Clearing data

Settings includes Clear All Data. After you confirm, the app deletes on-device progress and settings and does not restore them.
