# Content rating answers

Package: `com.studio.deadcityescape`

Dead City Escape is a violent game. It is not a Families app. There is no account. Answers below match the current build: zombie combat, no login, data stays on the device.

## Violence

- Does the game contain violence? **Yes.**
- Can the player fight or defeat characters? **Yes.** The player uses melee and firearms against zombie enemies and can die.
- Are the targets humans? **No.** Targets are zombies in a ruined city, not real people.
- Blood or gore? **No.** The build does not show realistic blood, dismemberment, or torture.
- How often does combat appear? **Frequent** during a run. Combat is the way through the city and the escape.
- Sexual violence? **No.**

Suggested Play questionnaire result: violence is present and fantasy-horror, without realistic blood. Do not mark the app as suitable for young children. A Teen or Mature rating is the expected band; confirm the final IARC result in Play Console. Do not enroll the app in the Families program.

## Other categories

- Sexuality: **No.**
- Language: **No** strong language is required to play.
- Controlled substances: **No.**
- Gambling: **No** real-money gambling and no loot boxes.
- User interaction: **No** chat, no account, no public profile.
- Shares location: **No.**
- Shares personal information: **No.** Data stays on the device and is not sent off the device.
- Horror or fear: **Yes, mild to moderate.** Dark city and hostile zombies.
- Crude humor: **No.**

## Audience

- Target age: not children. Treat as 16+ unless the console rating says otherwise.
- Families / Designed for Families: **No.**
- Ads, IAP, or account gate: **No.**
