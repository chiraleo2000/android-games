# Learning Gen Alpha device checklist

Run this on a phone after a debug or release install. Solo play is the store entry and must not wait on a server.

1. Launch the app. The 3D island appears immediately. There is no server connection screen.
2. Move with the on-screen pad. The avatar walks in the existing world.
3. Walk to a colored beacon. A puzzle opens with choices.
4. Submit a wrong answer. The puzzle stays open.
5. Use Hint at least once, then submit the correct answer. The score increases and the beacon changes.
6. Solve the remaining puzzles until the finished line says all puzzles are finished.
7. Close the app and open it again. The score and solved puzzles are still there.
8. Open Settings. The privacy policy link uses the in-app URL constant.
9. Choose Clear All Data, cancel once, then confirm. The app says the data was cleared.
10. Close the app and open it again. The old score is gone.
11. If the world cannot start, the fallback screen offers Try again and does not show a blank page.
