# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how [LEGAL_NAME] ("we") handles information in the Word Blitz app (package `com.wordblitz.kids`).

## On-device scores only

Word Blitz can save game scores on your device, including the language and topic you picked. Typed answers and scores stay on the device. They are not logged or transmitted off the device.

## No account, no ads, no off-device transmission

- We do not ask anyone to create an account.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- We do not sell or share personal information.
- The app does not transmit scores, typed words, or any other player information off the device.

## Android INTERNET permission

The Android package includes the `INTERNET` permission because the Capacitor Android WebView loads the bundled game from a local `https://localhost` address inside the app. Android requires that permission for the WebView's local loader. The permission is not used to contact our servers or any third party, and the game is playable without a network connection.

## Children

Word Blitz is a kids typing game for learning English and Spanish words. It does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else.

## Clearing scores

Settings and the game-over screen include Clear saved scores. After you confirm, the app deletes on-device scores and does not restore them.
