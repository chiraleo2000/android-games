## The app

Word Blitz is an offline kids typing game in English and Spanish. You look at a picture clue and type the matching word. Saved scores stay on your device. There is no account.

## Acceptable use

## No ads and no off-device transmission

The app does not show ads. It does not send scores, typed words, or other player data off the device.

## Saved scores

You can clear saved scores from Settings or the game-over screen. After you confirm, they are deleted from the device and are not restored.
