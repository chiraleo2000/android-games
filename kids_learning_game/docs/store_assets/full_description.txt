Word Blitz is an offline typing game for kids. Pick English or Spanish, pick a topic, and type the word that matches the picture clue. A 10-second streak raises the score. You have five minutes.

Topics include animals, food, colors, vehicles, sports, body parts, nature, household, clothing, and school. The game keeps both languages.

Scores can be saved on the device. Open Settings, or the game-over screen, to clear saved scores. The app asks you to confirm, then deletes them and does not restore them.

There is no account, no ads, and no off-device transmission of scores or typed words. Word Blitz works without a network connection. The privacy policy and terms are linked inside the app.
