## Your device

You can clear career results, personality results, progress, and preferences in Settings after you confirm. You can export or import a backup file on the device. Android `INTERNET` is declared for the Capacitor WebView. `FileProvider` is `exported="false"` and is used for that local export and import. With `allowBackup` enabled, Android may include the app's data in the user's own Google backup.
