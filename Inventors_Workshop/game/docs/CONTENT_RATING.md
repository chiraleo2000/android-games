# Content rating questionnaire — Inventors Workshop

Package: `com.inventorsworkshop.app`

Answers for the Google Play content-rating questionnaire. This is an educational workshop for children and families. It has no accounts, no ads, and no online play.

## Violence

- Cartoon or fantasy violence: No
- Realistic violence: No
- Blood or gore: No
- Weapons that can be aimed at people: No

## Sexuality

- Sexual content, nudity, or suggestive themes: No

## Language

- Profanity or crude language: No

## Controlled substances

- Alcohol, tobacco, or drugs: No

## Miscellaneous

- Gambling, real or simulated: No
- Horror or fear themes: No
- User-to-user interaction, chat, or public profiles: No
- User-generated content shared with other users inside the app: No
- The player can export a JSON machine file and share that file themselves. The app has no community, no upload, and no feed.
- Location sharing: No
- Digital purchases or ads: No
- Unrestricted web browsing: No. The workshop does not open remote pages.

## Audience

- Designed as a children's educational app. Complete the Families declarations in Play Console after the legal placeholders are replaced.
- No violence rating exception is needed. This app is not a violent game and is intended for the Families program.
